## Description

Found by the fresh-context review of Phase 5 (advisory finding 3) and settled by the Iteration's spike (`evidence/p5-spike-recipe.kt.txt`, output `evidence/p5-spike-output.txt`): assumption A-011 said Robolectric may not deliver a gesture's progress to Navigation 3, and nobody had tried. It does. `OnBackPressedDispatcher.dispatchOnBackStarted`, `dispatchOnBackProgressed` and `dispatchOnBackCancelled` (public, `@VisibleForTesting`) with a `BackEventCompat` reach `NavDisplay`, whose seek then moves the screens. Observed at width 320 px: at progress 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5, 0.8 the top screen's left edge is 16, 32, 64, 96, 160, 256 (exactly progress times width); the screen underneath is composed from the first frames (left -58 at 0.05, about 0 by 0.2); a cancel brings the top screen back in about 0.85 s and leaves only it; a commit (`onBackPressed()`) pops the back stack at once and the top screen carries on at a constant speed out of the right edge, gone about 0.7 s later.

Write a test that drives this. It is the machine pre-check of DoD 21 step 8 (the person still judges how it feels) and the machine proof of T-014's claim.
