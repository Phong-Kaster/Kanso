## Description

Each tool opens its own screen (Q15): one `ToolScreen` parameterised by the key's tool, no switching.

- **Layout** (top to bottom, `Scaffold` or a plain column; Material 3 Expressive; every colour from the theme): a top bar with a `RoundBackButton` at the top left (`tool-back`) and the tool's name centred (`Tool.labelRes`; it is the ONLY text on the screen equal to a tool label, PROJECT.md Constraint); a large rounded card `tool-card` (`MaterialTheme.shapes.extraLarge`, full width) holding the photo (`tool-image`, `ContentScale.Fit`, filling the card in one direction with the node's aspect ratio equal to the photo's) over a `BlurredBackdrop` that fills the rest of the card, with a floating `ElevatedAssistChip(shape = CircleShape)` "Replace" (`tool-replace`, icon `ic_swap_horiz`, new string `tool_screen_replace`) at the bottom of the card, 16 dp from its edge; under the card the options strip: the existing per-tool placeholder text `editor_panel_*` from `strings_tool.xml` in a rounded container tagged `editor-panel`; at the bottom a full-width `Button` "Save" (`tool-save`, medium height, `shapes =`), padded by `WindowInsets.navigationBars.union(WindowInsets.systemGestures.only(WindowInsetsSides.Bottom))` through `windowInsetsPadding` (PROJECT.md bottom-bar Constraint). No toolbar, no toggle, no `selectTool`, no `keptToolName`, no `onSelectTool`.
