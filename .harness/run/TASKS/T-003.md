## Description

Phase 3 of the PRD: the sticker on the photo - drawing, the frame and its four buttons, the gestures. Port from
`reference/pinity-stickers/java/` (read it, never import it): the drawing of a sticker and its frame
(`DecalOverlayPreview.kt`), the one-finger drag and two-finger pinch/twist of `DecalOverlayPointer.kt` and the
geometry helpers of `Offset.kt` / `KotlinUtil.kt` - rewritten to PLAN.md's Overlay contract: one overlay-level
raw pointer handler, newest-first hit test, no halving of zoom/pan/rotation, no tap-to-swap, `previewSettings`
per move and `commitSettings` on the last finger up; the overlay inside `PhotoCard`'s `TOOL_IMAGE_TAG` box,
clipped to it, active only while Stickers is open, Compare is off and not finishing. Corners: top left deletes;
top right duplicates (+4% of the shown width right and down, through `StickerMapping`) and selects the copy;
bottom left mirrors; bottom right resizes by distance and turns by angle about the centre, one handle. Corner
buttons are `StickerCornerButton`s: a `MaterialTheme.colorScheme` disc, 48 dp touch target, the Wiring Worker's
white-fill icon via `Icon(painterResource(...))`, a content description from `strings_sticker_frame.xml`.
Selection: tap selects, tap on empty photo selects none, closing the panel selects none, a key that no longer
exists (after undo, delete or any settings change) is pruned. All conversion between the shown picture and the
original goes through `StickerMapping` (T-002). Until Phase 4 the overlay draws the sticker pictures itself
(clipped to the shown picture, unfiltered). Every composable has light and dark previews from sample data; KDoc
teaches pointer input and why one handler hit-tests every sticker.
