## Description

- Back, flash (auto / on / off) and switch-lens become Expressive icon buttons drawn from the Symbols drawables (`ic_arrow_back`, `ic_flash_auto`, `ic_flash_on`, `ic_flash_off`, `ic_cameraswitch`) with `Icon(painterResource(...))`; no visible text; content descriptions kept exactly (`camera_flash_*` is read as the mode label by `CameraControlsTest:125-126`; `camera_switch_lens`; "Back"); at least 48 dp; Expressive `shapes` where the overload exists.
- Keep and Retake use icons (`ic_check`, `ic_refresh`) plus their existing texts and keep their tags and 48 dp size. The shutter keeps its tag `camera-shutter` and answers the press: an inner node shrinks or morphs while pressed (a `MutableInteractionSource` pressed state driving `animateFloatAsState` / the motion scheme) and returns on release; a tap still takes exactly one picture.
- The picture area and every surface follow the theme: no `Color.Black` anywhere in `ui/camera` (R6); letterbox and unavailable areas use a theme colour role. The live feed itself is unaffected. If the camera shows a visible `Starting` state, show `LoadingIndicator` there (DoD 9).
- **Keep frozen:** `LivePreview` and `ShotView` (exempt by file and name), the previews `CameraLive`, `CameraAfterShot`, `CameraUnavailable`, `CameraRefused` (names), `CameraContent`'s parameters, all `camera-*` tags, the `R.string.tool_camera` title. `AccessRefusedPanel` is called as it is today.
- Bottom controls clear the navigation bar and gesture insets. Tests: paused clock (`mainClock.autoAdvance = false`) for the press; a new `CameraControlsScreenshotTest` that renders `CameraContent` with a drawn picture, light and dark, for pictures 20 and 21 (new, recorded by the Iteration); `CameraRefusedScreenshotTest` re-records 14 and 15.
