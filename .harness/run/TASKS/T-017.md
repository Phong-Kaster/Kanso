## Description

Found by the second fresh-context review of Phase 5 (advisory 1 and 5, both worth a task). Opacity was T-014's whole point, and no test checks it: put `fadeOut(animationSpec = fade)` back on the top screen's exit in `kansoPredictivePopTransition` and all three `PredictiveBackGestureTest` tests still pass (positions do not change and a fading screen is still composed). And the commit test only checks "moves right each frame, then is gone": a change that snaps the screen away at once after the release would pass.

1. `PredictiveBackOpacityTest` (new class, `@GraphicsMode(GraphicsMode.Mode.NATIVE)` so that drawing the content view into a bitmap paints real pixels, as `ToolSelectionAnimationTest` does; never `captureToImage()`): the host of `PredictiveBackGestureTest` (copy its helpers) but with two solid-colour screens (for example Home a plain red-ish colour, Settings a plain blue-ish colour, `Modifier.background(...)`, in the test file: plain `Color` values are fine in a test), the gesture started and moved to progress 0.3; then read two pixels from the drawn content view: one at a point inside the top screen's region (right of its left edge, vertically in the middle) and one at a point left of the top screen's left edge (inside the screen underneath). The first must equal the top screen's colour exactly (opaque; with a fade-out it would have blended to the colour underneath within the first tenth of the swipe), the second the colour of the screen underneath (it is drawn behind, and in place by progress 0.2). Also at progress 0.8 the pixel inside the top screen's remaining strip (if the strip is wide enough) is still the top colour. Compare ARGB values with a small per-channel tolerance only if exact equality fails for a reason you can name (anti-aliasing at the edge: sample away from the edge).
2. `PredictiveBackGestureTest`, the commit test: after the release, assert that the last left edge seen before the top screen disappears is at least the window width minus two frame steps of the slide (name the constant; the slide is a straight line of about 10 px per 32 ms at 320 px, so the last position is within about 10 px of the width), and that the whole finish takes about as long as the straight-line slide leaves (at progress 0.3 about 44 frames after the release, assert the number of frames is within a generous band, for example 30 to 60, named constants), so a change that snaps the screen away would fail.
3. The KDoc of `PredictiveBackGestureTest` (class and test 3) says the events travel "the same road as a real gesture" and "the way Android hands a gesture to the app": reword to what is true. The test sends the events into the activity's `OnBackPressedDispatcher` through its test functions (`dispatchOnBackStarted`, `dispatchOnBackProgressed`, `dispatchOnBackCancelled`, and `onBackPressed` for the release), which stand in for the system; from there they reach NavDisplay as a gesture's events do. On a phone the system's events enter the dispatcher through another input (the platform's back callback), so what this test cannot check is that handoff from the system to the dispatcher; the manifest flag is proved by the `aapt2` check of DoD 14, and a person checks the real swipe (DoD 21 step 8). Test 3's KDoc says the top screen "neither vanishes nor jumps back": say what is checked ("still composed", and from the new assertion, reaches the right edge).
