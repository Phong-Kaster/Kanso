## Description

Replace Edit's all-at-once 260 dp strip: a fixed photo (60% of the space below the top bar) that never resizes; under it a horizontal LazyRow of four entries (icon over name: Crop, Rotate, Adjust, Filters; `EditTool` enum in `data/edit/edit/`), each opening a NON-modal bottom sheet filling the remaining 40% with no scrim. A floating pill at the photo's bottom right holds Undo (the existing undo) and a Compare switch (shows the original, disables every editing control). The Replace chip is gone from Edit only. The sheets hold the existing controls regrouped (Crop: ratio chips + the two range sliders for now; Rotate: 4 buttons; Adjust: 4 sliders; Filters: chips + intensity); they still apply the old one-change way - the settings model is T-002. Open sheet and comparing live in ToolUiState (one StateFlow). Row entries highlight while open. Icons: the Iteration adds the drawables first. Every composable has light and dark previews; stateless Content takes UiState + callbacks.
